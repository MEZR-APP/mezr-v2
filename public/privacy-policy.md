# Mezr Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 6, 2025**

---

## What We Collect

We only collect what is essential to run your account and improve your experience. That means your **email address** (for account login and recovery) and **breathing session times**—such as when you did a session and how long you breathed. We do not need more than that.

We do **not** collect:
- Your location
- Device information
- Health data from your device
- Behavior tracking
- Advertising IDs

We do not build a profile of you for ads or analytics. What you do in the app stays between you and the app unless you choose to back it up to the cloud.

---

## Where Your Data Lives

**By default, everything stays on your device.** Your sessions are saved locally, you keep full control of your data, and the app works offline. We believe you should own your data and decide where it goes.

**Cloud backup is optional.** You choose whether to enable it in Settings. If you do, your session data is encrypted during transit and stored securely in Firebase so you can access it from multiple devices. We do not use your cloud data for anything other than giving you access to it when you want it.

---

## Your Rights

You can:
- **View all your data** (Privacy Dashboard: Settings → Privacy Dashboard)
- **Download your data** (Export button)
- **Delete everything** (Delete Account)

We do not make it hard to leave or to take your data with you.

---

## No Tracking

We do **not**:
- Track your behavior inside or outside the app
- Show ads
- Sell your data to anyone
- Share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising

Our business is not built on your personal information.
